Turn on notifications and act on one

Gist

Why: with the app closed nothing told the owner that new to-dos arrived or that something is due today, so due work was missed unless they remembered to open the app.

What you will see: turn notifications on once from the Assistant sheet in the Home Screen app and allow them. New to-dos from the assistant and a 7:00 am summary on days with something due then arrive while the app is closed, and tapping either opens Habit Tracker on Today. Only due dates leave the phone, never titles. Declining shows where to turn notifications on in Settings, and Turn off notifications stops them.

Journey

  1. Open Habit Tracker from the Home Screen.
  2. Hold the version line on the Lists tab for 3 seconds.
  3. The Assistant sheet shows Connected and a Notifications section.
  4. Tap Turn on notifications.
  5. The iPhone asks to allow notifications.
  6. Allow: the sheet says notifications are on.
  7. Don't Allow: the sheet says they are blocked and where to turn them on in Settings.
  8. Close the app.
  9. The assistant sends a to-do: a notification names the new to-dos.
  10. 7:00 am on a day with something due: a summary lists due and overdue to-dos.
  11. Tap the notification.
  12. Habit Tracker opens on Today with the to-dos synced.
  13. Later: Turn off notifications, and nothing more arrives.
